# Non-Functional Requirements

This document lists the non-functional requirements for the Employee Leave Management System.

## NFR-001 — Performance
The system shall respond to user actions (e.g. submitting a leave request) within 2 seconds under normal load.

## NFR-002 — Availability
The system shall be available 99.5% of the time during business hours.

## NFR-003 — Security
The system shall restrict access to leave data based on user role (Employee, Manager, HR Administrator).

## NFR-004 — Usability
The system shall be usable by employees with no prior training, using clear labels and guided workflows.

## NFR-005 — Auditability
The system shall retain an audit trail of leave request actions for a minimum of 3 years.

## NFR-006 — Data Privacy
The system shall handle employee personal and leave data in compliance with applicable data protection regulations.
