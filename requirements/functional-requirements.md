# Functional Requirements

This document lists the functional requirements for the Employee Leave Management System.

## FR-001
The system shall allow an employee to submit a leave request, including leave type, start date, end date and reason.

## FR-002
The system shall allow a manager to approve or reject leave requests submitted by their direct reports.

## FR-003
The system shall allow HR administrators to configure leave types and leave balances.

## FR-004
The system shall calculate and display the number of leave days requested based on the selected date range.

## FR-005
The system shall allow employees to view the status of their submitted leave requests.

## FR-006
The system shall send a notification when a leave request is submitted, approved, or rejected.

## FR-007
The system shall prevent an employee from submitting overlapping leave requests.

## FR-008
The system shall maintain an audit trail of all leave request actions (submitted, approved, rejected, cancelled).
